Mercer Island School District

A six-month longitudinal analysis examining standardized assessments, attendance patterns, and course performance across roughly 4,000 K–12 students to help district educators identify early indicators of academic growth and support needs.

1. Context & Goals

School districts collect rich longitudinal records across multiple assessment platforms, attendance logs, and course transcripts—yet those sources often live in separate silos. Working alongside Mercer Island School District stakeholders from January to June 2026, our goal was to unify these records and examine how early assessment benchmarks and attendance patterns relate to downstream student outcomes.

2. Data & Privacy

The analytical dataset combined de-identified longitudinal records across roughly 4,000 students, spanning:

  • Standardized Assessments: Smarter Balanced Assessment Consortium (SBAC), i-Ready diagnostic scores, and Cognitive Abilities Test (CogAT) records.
  • Engagement & Coursework: Multi-year attendance rates, course grades, and demographic indicators.
Data Governance & FERPA Compliance

All student identifiers were removed prior to analysis, and any charts or summary metrics shared publicly are aggregated at the cohort level to protect student privacy.
